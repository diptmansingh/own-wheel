1. Rental Agreement

By booking a vehicle through Own Wheel Car Rentals, you agree to enter into a rental agreement subject to these Terms and Conditions. The rental period begins at the scheduled pickup time and ends at the scheduled return time.

2. Eligibility Requirements

To rent a vehicle from Own Wheel, you must:

  • Be at least 21 years of age
  • Hold a valid Indian Driving License for at least 1 year
  • Possess a valid Aadhaar Card as ID proof
  • Have a local reference or hotel proof of stay in Lucknow
  • Pass our verification process

3. Booking & Payment

3.1 Advance Payment

A non-refundable advance fee of ₹500 is required to confirm your booking. This amount is adjusted against your total rental charges.

3.2 Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit of ₹2,500 is collected at vehicle pickup. This deposit is refunded within 3-5 business days after vehicle return, subject to inspection.

3.3 Rental Charges

Rental charges are calculated based on the selected duration and vehicle. All prices are inclusive of taxes. Extra kilometers beyond the 250 KM/day limit are charged at ₹8 per KM.

4. Vehicle Usage

The renter agrees to:

  • Use the vehicle only for lawful purposes
  • Not use the vehicle for racing, towing, or off-road driving
  • Not sublease or transfer the vehicle to any other person
  • Not drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs
  • Comply with all traffic laws and regulations
  • Return the vehicle with the same fuel level as at pickup

5. Insurance & Liability

Basic third-party insurance is included. The renter is responsible for:

  • Any damage caused by reckless driving or traffic violations
  • Traffic fines and penalties incurred during the rental period
  • Theft or loss of vehicle accessories
  • Damage caused by unauthorized drivers

6. Cancellation Policy

Please refer to our Cancellation Policy for detailed terms. In summary:

  • Cancellation 24+ hours before pickup: Full advance refund
  • Cancellation within 24 hours: 50% advance refund
  • No-show: No refund

7. Vehicle Return

The vehicle must be returned:

  • At the agreed location and time
  • In clean condition (normal wear accepted)
  • With the same fuel level as at pickup
  • With all original documents and accessories

Late returns are charged at 1.5x the hourly rate for each hour of delay.

8. Breakdown & Accidents

In case of breakdown or accident:

  • Contact our 24/7 helpline immediately: +91 93404 06464
  • Do not attempt unauthorized repairs
  • In case of accident, file an FIR if required
  • Cooperate with insurance assessment

9. Privacy

Your personal information is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We collect and use your data solely for booking verification and service improvement.

10. Governing Law

These Terms and Conditions are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.
