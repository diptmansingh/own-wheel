How It Works

Book your self-drive car in 4 simple steps

Home/How It Works
1

Select Your Car

Browse our fleet of premium vehicles and choose the one that fits your needs. We offer:

  • Maruti Dzire — Compact sedan, perfect for city drives
  • Tata Nexon — Spacious SUV for family trips
  • Hyundai i20 — Premium hatchback with automatic transmission

Each car comes with transparent pricing — no hidden charges ever.

2

Choose Dates & Fill Details

Select your pickup and return date/time. Then provide your personal information:

  • Full name and contact details
  • Valid email address for booking confirmation
  • Current address in Lucknow or hotel details
  • Purpose of rental (personal, business, wedding, tourism)

You must be 21 years or older with a valid driving license.

3

Upload Documents & Pay Advance

Upload the following documents for verification:

  • Aadhaar Card (front & back)
  • Valid Driving License
  • Recent live selfie/photo
  • Local reference or hotel proof

Pay a small ₹500 advance fee to secure your booking. Security deposit of ₹2,500 is paid at pickup.

4

Get Approved & Drive

Our team reviews your booking within minutes:

  • Receive an email confirmation once approved
  • Arrive 15 minutes before pickup time
  • Bring original documents and pay security deposit
  • Inspect the car, sign the agreement, and hit the road!

All cars include 250 KM/day limit. Extra KM charged at ₹8/km. 24/7 support available.
