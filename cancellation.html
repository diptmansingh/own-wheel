Cancellation Policy

Understand our cancellation and refund terms

Home/Cancellation Policy
Last Updated: May 19, 2026

1. Overview

At Own Wheel, we understand that plans can change. This Cancellation Policy outlines the terms for cancelling your booking and the applicable refund structure.

Important: All cancellations must be made by calling us at +91 93404 06464 or emailing bookings@ownwheel.com. Cancellations are effective from the time we confirm receipt.

2. Cancellation Timeframes & Refunds

The refund amount depends on when you cancel relative to your scheduled pickup time:

Cancellation Time Advance Fee Refund Security Deposit
24+ hours before pickup 100% Refund (₹500) Not applicable (not yet paid)
12-24 hours before pickup 50% Refund (₹250) Not applicable (not yet paid)
Less than 12 hours before pickup No Refund Not applicable (not yet paid)
No-show (without cancellation) No Refund Not applicable (not yet paid)

3. How to Cancel

You can cancel your booking through any of the following methods:

  • Phone: Call us at +91 93404 06464 (24/7)
  • Email: Send cancellation request to bookings@ownwheel.com
  • WhatsApp: Message us with your Booking ID

Please provide your Booking ID and registered phone number for verification.

4. Refund Processing

4.1 Refund Timeline

  • Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days from cancellation confirmation
  • The refund will be credited to the original payment method
  • UPI refunds typically reflect within 24-48 hours
  • Card refunds may take 5-7 business days depending on your bank

4.2 Refund Method

Refunds are always processed to the original payment method used during booking. We do not issue refunds to different accounts for security reasons.

5. Special Circumstances

5.1 Force Majeure

In case of unforeseen circumstances such as natural disasters, government lockdowns, or other force majeure events, we will:

  • Offer full refund or free rescheduling
  • Waive all cancellation fees
  • Provide priority rebooking when services resume

5.2 Vehicle Unavailability

If we are unable to provide the booked vehicle due to mechanical issues or other reasons:

  • We will offer a similar or upgraded vehicle at no extra cost
  • If no alternative is acceptable, full refund including advance fee
  • Compensation in the form of discount on next booking

5.3 Early Return

If you return the vehicle before the scheduled return time:

  • No refund for unused rental hours
  • Security deposit refund process begins immediately after inspection

6. Late Returns

If you need to extend your rental period:

  • Contact us at least 4 hours before scheduled return
  • Extension is subject to vehicle availability
  • Late returns without intimation are charged at 1.5x the hourly rate

7. Contact for Cancellations

For any cancellation-related queries:

  • Phone: +91 93404 06464 (24/7)
  • Email: bookings@ownwheel.com
  • WhatsApp: Available on request

We value your trust. If you have any concerns about our cancellation policy, please don't hesitate to reach out. We're here to help.
